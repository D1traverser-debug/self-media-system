Premium editorial illustration for a sharp crypto publication. Human, contemporary and observant, with tension but no glossy token-ad aesthetic. ## 6. Inline visual 03 — three ledgers, one word

**Canvas:** 16:9 horizontal, 1600 × 900 px.  
**Placement:** After Section 05.  
**Caption:** “Culture, ownership and cash flow are three different ledgers.”  
**Alt text:** Three connected but distinct groups represent cultural contributors, token owners and fee recipients.

**Prompt:**

Create a clean, sophisticated editorial illustration in 16:9 landscape format. The concept is the three ledgers hidden inside the word “community.”

Show three connected but distinct visual zones: cultural contribution, with an artist, remixers, translators, event organizers and moderators working with one shared abstract meme glyph; token ownership, with people holding differently sized abstract token claims; and cash-flow rights, with separate destinations receiving creator fees or holder rewards through a simple, legible flow.

Some people may overlap between zones, but do not collapse all roles into one generic crowd. Make the distinction immediately visible: the people creating cultural recognition, owning token supply and receiving fees are not necessarily the same people.

Avoid moral judgment. Early buyers may have taken risk; creators and community builders may have contributed labor; fee recipients may be defined by the token's configuration. Clarify the different claims without declaring one group good and another bad.

Use the shared palette and editorial style. Leave clean negative space for three labels to be added manually.

No text, numbers, percentages, logos, tickers, watermarks, wallet addresses, coin piles, recognizable existing meme characters or generic neon Web3 backgrounds.
