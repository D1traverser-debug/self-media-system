## 4. Five-post thread

**1/5**  
A meme can belong to the internet. The token built around it belongs to its holders. Those are two different claims.

**2/5**  
Chill Guy's creator didn't launch the token. Traders were asking him to post a Solana address so they could send him money or tokens. Same image, different interests.

**3/5**  
Bitquery checked 8.2M Pump.fun launches: developer wallets appeared in block 0 on 21% of launches; the dev's first sale came within a minute on 65%. Wallet classifications, not a scam rate.

**4/5**  
A holder can mean every word and still want new buyers. Sincerity doesn't remove the incentive; the incentive doesn't prove the person is lying.

**5/5**  
My test: does the culture keep going when the chart goes flat? Can you see who owns the supply and where fees go? “Community” isn't an answer to either question.
