## Source verification and wording limits

### 1. Chill Guy / Phillip Banks
CoinDesk reported on November 21, 2024 that Banks said he would issue takedown notices for unauthorized for-profit uses, particularly merchandise and shitcoins, while saying he was less concerned about brands using the character as a trend. CoinDesk also reported that the CHILLGUY token creator was unknown and that traders were asking Banks to post a Solana address.
- Source: https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2024/11/21/chillguy-creator-threatens-legal-action-as-crypto-trenches-scour-tiktok
- Limit: This article reports Banks's statement; it is not a court ruling on ownership or infringement.

### 2. Jais & White (2026)
The study uses daily data from 2021 through March 2026 and reports turnover sensitivity to salient price movements, intra-community return spillovers, illiquidity and downside tail risk.
- Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0275531926003491
- Limit: These are market-level empirical patterns. They do not prove that a particular post caused a particular price move.

### 3. Bitquery Pump.fun sample
Bitquery examined 8,212,568 launches whose first trades occurred between October 30, 2025 and October 7, 2026. It reports developer-linked wallets buying in the launch block on 21% of launches and a developer first sale within one minute on 65% of launches.
- Source: https://www.bitquery.io/investigations/pump-fun-snipers-bundles
- Limit: Wallet linkage is inferred from on-chain transaction sequence and funding signals, not verified real-world identity. A first sale is not proof of a complete exit. The percentages are not scam rates.

### 4. Pump.fun fees
Pump.fun documentation states that a creator may choose at launch to direct the Creator fee to eligible holders instead of the creator. Holder Rewards are subject to eligibility rules, distribution costs and platform conditions; distributions are not guaranteed.
- Source: https://pump.fun/docs/fees
- Limit: This is a platform-specific configuration, not a universal memecoin rule or an automatic royalty to the original meme artist.

### 5. Dogecoin Dogesled fundraiser
PBS reported on January 21, 2014 that the Dogesled fundraiser had raised more than $30,000 in Dogecoin for Jamaica's two-man bobsled team to travel to Sochi.
- Source: https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/jamaican-bobsled-team-raises-30000-in-dogecoin-for-trip-to-sochi
- Limit: The fundraiser demonstrates real coordination. It does not establish that another meme token is fairly priced or distributes value fairly.
