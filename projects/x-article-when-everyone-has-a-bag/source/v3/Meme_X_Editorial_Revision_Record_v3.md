## Root-cause diagnosis

The v2 candidate had a sound central observation---financial incentives
make public conviction ambiguous---but did not fully convert it into a
useful model for the reader. It repeated the ambiguity across sections,
then ended with a broad question. It explained why a timeline can
mislead, but did not clearly state what a better market should make
visible or what the next durable memecoin should get right.

This is a `THESIS_PAYOFF / ARGUMENT_PROGRESS` defect, not a need for
more source volume.

## Selected revision

Preserve the approved thesis and sharpen it through three ledgers: 1.
Cultural contribution --- who creates, remixes, translates, moderates
and organizes? 2. Token ownership --- who received supply, when, how
concentrated is it, and who can sell into new attention? 3. Cash-flow
rights --- who receives creator fees, holder rewards, referral payments
or other recurring income?

**The market's central confusion is calling everyone "the community"
when they contribute, own and earn in very different ways.**

The article then states a clear standard for the next durable memecoin:
culture that survives a flat chart plus an economic engine with legible
ownership and fee flows.
