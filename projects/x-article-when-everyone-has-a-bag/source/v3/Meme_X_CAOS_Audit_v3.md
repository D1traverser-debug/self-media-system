# CAOS Audit v3 --- Meme X Article

Date: 2026-11-21 Status: refined candidate; not published.

## Selected position

**The market's central confusion is calling everyone "the community"
when they contribute, own and earn in very different ways.**

The approved CAOS thesis remains: financial incentives make public
conviction an ambiguous signal. The v3 article explains that ambiguity
through three separate ledgers: cultural contribution, token ownership
and cash-flow rights.

## Evidence used

1.  **Chill Guy:** CoinDesk reported on Nov. 21, 2024 that Phillip Banks
    said he would issue takedowns for unauthorized for-profit uses
    including shitcoins; the token creator was unknown, and traders
    asked Banks to post a Solana address so they could send him
    tokens. https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2024/11/21/chillguy-creator-threatens-legal-action-as-crypto-trenches-scour-tiktok

2.  **Attention feedback:** Jais & White's 2026 study, using daily data
    from 2021 through March 2026, reports turnover sensitivity to
    salient price moves and intra-segment return spillovers in
    community-driven cryptoassets. It supports a feedback mechanism, not
    proof that a particular rally was
    manufactured. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0275531926003491

3.  **Early token positions:** Bitquery examined 8,212,568 Pump.fun
    launches whose first trades occurred between Oct. 30, 2025 and
    Oct. 7, 2026. It reports developer-linked wallets buying in block 0
    on 21% of launches and a developer first sale within one minute on
    65%. These are methodology-dependent classifications, not scam
    rates. https://www.bitquery.io/investigations/pump-fun-snipers-bundles

4.  **Fee recipients:** Pump.fun documentation distinguishes Creator
    Fees and Holder Rewards. For eligible token configurations, a
    creator may route fees to holders rather than a creator wallet. This
    is a platform-specific example, not a universal memecoin
    rule. https://pump.fun/docs/fees

5.  **Positive counterexample:** PBS reported that Dogecoin's Dogesled
    fundraiser raised more than \$30,000 for Jamaica's bobsled team to
    travel to the 2014 Sochi Winter
    Olympics. https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/jamaican-bobsled-team-raises-30000-in-dogecoin-for-trip-to-sochi

6.  **X Article presentation:** X's official guide supports headings,
    subheadings, bold, links, images and lists. It recommends short
    paragraphs, subheadings every 3--5 paragraphs, bold key insights,
    and ruthless editing. The 5:2 and 16:9 image dimensions are
    production choices, not official mandatory
    ratios. https://help.x.com/en/using-x/articles

## Sentence-level editorial checks

-   Every section advances a distinct step: cultural object vs token;
    attention-price feedback; early supply; mixed motives; three
    ledgers; positive counterexample; future standard. - Replaced
    abstract or repeated transitions with concrete claims and causal
    links. - Removed repeated defensive caveats; retained only
    qualifications needed to prevent misreading of the evidence. - No
    unsupported claim that all holders are promoters, all early wallets
    are fraudulent, or all memecoin communities are fake. - No claim
    that market cap equals cash invested or executable exit liquidity. -
    No claim that Creator Fees automatically reward the original meme
    artist. - No use of a chart or wallet count as a direct measure of
    unique human community members. - English and Chinese versions
    preserve the same title, thesis, seven sections and five source
    URLs.
