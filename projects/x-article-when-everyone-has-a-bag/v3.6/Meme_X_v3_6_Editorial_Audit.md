## C. Editorial decision and structural repair

### Why the new opening is a better editorial opportunity

v3.5 opened with an abstract value proposition after a short quip. v3.6 restores the remarkable actual conflict: a trader proposing 2% of a token to an artist who had not launched it, while that token had reportedly approached a $500m market capitalization and then fallen sharply. The source documents the trader's joke, the creator's quoted reaction and the market-cap arc. The article derives its argument from the opposing *roles*, not a fictitious debate.

### Reader value acquired in sequence

1. **Open:** The cultural author and the token issuer may be different people. Monetary “compensation” cannot automatically settle cultural/IP concerns.
2. **Culture + attention:** A joke spreads socially; tokens have specified ownership. Price screenshots become another distribution channel. A busy feed and sell-side depth are not equivalent.
3. **Trading timeline:** Original wallets can be trading from the first seconds, while the public hears of the coin later. The timing difference changes what enthusiastic posts mean to a potential buyer.
4. **Contribution + payouts:** Artwork, moderation and fundraising can be real social value. Supply distribution and fee parameters specify economic recipients. One person may occupy multiple roles but shared language does not reveal the overlap.
5. **Payoff:** Celebrate cultural participation while reading ownership and incentives as separate evidence. The last two paragraphs return to the original Chill Guy clash.

The visible edition uses three section headings; the five beats above are *private editorial logic*, not a mandated public list or question inventory.

### Sentence-level fixes that matter

- Removed the v3.5 passage defining opportunism as fraudulent self-interest. It was a topic detour and could misleadingly collapse disclosure ethics, promotion and creative opportunism.
- Replaced generic value language (“the cultural record lives in...”; “the ownership record lives in...”) with named behaviors and concrete market outcomes.
- Added the specific source-backed reported ~$500m cap and near-half decline where readers first encounter the conflict.
- Rebuilt the Bitquery section around two timelines, keeping its sampling and inference limits adjacent to the figures.
- Preserved mixed motives without flat moral binaries: creative labor can be genuine and financially incentivized.
- Reworked the conclusion to connect the author's control over a character with traders offering supply, then state the lasting interpretive point.
- No body questions were introduced; the single question mark belongs to the retained original title.
