## Shared look

High-end editorial illustration with a distinct hand-drawn intellectual character. Restrained ink/graphite, parchment off-white, deep indigo and a single sharp vermilion accent. Paper texture and fine linework rather than glossy Web3 gradients. Editorial restraint, human interaction, legibility and negative space. Avoid copied Chill Guy likeness, known meme imagery, exchange logos, charts labeled as actual market records, rocket motifs and generic stacks of coins. No machine-generated text or statistics within the illustration.

### Header artwork — Cultural reach vs. financial allocation

**Canvas:** 5:2 horizontal, recommended working size 2000×800.  
**Placement:** X Article cover.  
**Image alt text:** A small anonymous drawing spreads through many hands and screens, while tokens and fee receipts collect in a much narrower set of separate wallets.  
**Art prompt:**

Create an original contemporary editorial illustration for a thoughtful essay on internet memes, memecoins and the uneasy overlap of cultural popularity and financial rewards. Use a wide 5:2 composition. A single abstract hand-drawn glyph moves through a loose crowd of artists, fans and screens; close to the right, the same glyph enters a restrained token-market visual language, where separate wallets hold unequal amounts and a fee stream reaches only a few recipients. The crowd and holders should overlap in places; there are no cartoon villains. The visual metaphor is shared attention versus allocated economic benefit. Refined line art, tactile newsprint grain, warm paper, deep indigo, ink black and small vermilion accents. Strong central silhouette, space at the edges for responsive crops, and clean upper-center negative space. No typography, readable UI, numbers, logos, recognizable meme characters, or existing intellectual property.

### Inline A — The price screenshot becomes part of the story

**Canvas:** 16:9 horizontal, 1600×900.  
**Placement:** after the paragraph ending `no single person directing it.`  
**Caption:** *A price move becomes a post. The post brings more eyes to the trade.*  
**Image alt text:** An abstract rising price chart is photographed and reshared through a social feed, drawing more viewers and showing a feedback loop between attention and trading.  
**Art prompt:**

Illustrate a contemporary social feedback loop in one continuous 16:9 editorial composition. An abstract rising market line is captured on a phone as a screenshot. It travels visually through several phones and social posts, attracting fresh eyes and returning to the original market signal. Show motion through layered depth, compositional echoes and gaze direction, not a numbered process infographic. Keep characters believable, mixed in motive and style; the image should show an emergent market behavior rather than a secret centrally coordinated plot. Refined hand-drawn ink on textured off-white, deep indigo and vermilion highlights. Generic abstract interface geometry only. No fabricated data labels or numbers, no exchange logos, no rockets, no typography.

### Inline B — The public feed and the launch clock

**Canvas:** 16:9 horizontal, 1600×900.  
**Placement:** after the paragraph ending `completely different entry prices and reasons to act.`  
**Caption:** *The launch clock and the public discovery clock rarely start together.*  
**Image alt text:** Two timelines show wallets trading in the first moments of a token's launch while another group discovers the ticker later through social posts.  
**Art prompt:**

A premium 16:9 editorial illustration juxtaposes two different time experiences of the same memecoin. In a slightly recessed left layer, small wallet-like figures act around an abstract launch moment, with transactions happening immediately. In the brighter foreground right layer, an ordinary reader encounters the same anonymous glyph as it appears through a trending feed later. A shared visual motif connects both timelines while their starting points are plainly different. Preserve ambiguity of motive: early position is not automatic fraud and later discovery is not automatic naïveté. Hand-drawn precision, newsprint texture, warm off-white, indigo, graphite and controlled vermilion. Leave lower whitespace for source-verified typesetting if needed. No readable text, wallet addresses, real exchange UI, token logos, percentages or fictitious statistics.

**Optional manual label under figure B (do not embed within image generation):** `Bitquery, 8,212,568 Pump.fun launches (first trades: Oct. 30, 2025–Oct. 7, 2026): developer's first sale within 60 seconds on 65% of launches. Developer association is inferred from on-chain patterns; first sale does not imply a full exit.`
