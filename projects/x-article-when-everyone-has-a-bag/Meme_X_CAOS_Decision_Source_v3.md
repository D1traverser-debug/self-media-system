## 5. Reader argument / manuscript dependency graph
Central question: **When a whole community has financial exposure, how can a newcomer distinguish authentic cultural enthusiasm from economically motivated promotion?**
Central stance: earnestness and self-interest can coexist; the feedback between price and attention makes them difficult to distinguish; contributions to culture, ownership of token supply and fee entitlement are distinct; the result alters trust and value allocation.

1. Chill Guy: the artist and token promoters did not choose the same market; concrete origin conflict.
2. A meme vs ticker: recognition is not a token investment claim.
3. Price↔attention: price can become its own advertisement, but not all attention is fake.
4. Opening block: early economic rights do not begin when public narrative reaches later buyer.
5. Real community: sincere belief and incentive can coexist, including volunteer effort.
6. Value distribution and fee rules: the person creating cultural recognition may not be the holder capturing upside; distinguish economic actors.
7. Dogecoin bobsled counterexample: finance can help a real community achieve an external outcome.
8. Trust payoff: chart and social enthusiasm cannot independently certify one another; fairer participation question invites nontrivial disagreement.
