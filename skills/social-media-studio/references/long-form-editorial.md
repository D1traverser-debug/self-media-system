# Long-form Editorial

> Status: CANDIDATE_REQUIRES_REVALIDATION. This candidate combines reader-value and narrative-architecture mechanisms with evidence-derived checks from historical long-form work. It is not a validated universal formula. It does not import finance-specific assumptions, fiction-specific plot formulas, proprietary expression or a fixed chapter template.

## Scope

Long-form articles, essays, X Articles and other substantial editorial pieces. Do not apply this structure to a one-line caption or turn an article into a video-production pipeline.

## Reader contract before prose

Define the target reader and their job; the article promise and central question; the author's actual thesis or point of view; a concrete opening anchor; the information gap that makes the piece worth entering; an early payoff that proves substance; and a later payoff that makes completion worthwhile.

The article should give a real reader a reason to enter, a clear path through the material, repeated information gain and an ending that pays off the promise.

**Thesis test without the headline:** hide the title and ask what the article actually argues. If the remaining manuscript is only an event recap, a familiar slogan or a source catalogue, rebuild the thesis before polishing sentences.

## Thesis, sources and claim boundaries

Build a claim-to-source ledger before relying on research. For each material factual claim, record the exact source, what it directly supports, what it does not support, relevant date/context, uncertainty and any meaningful counterevidence.

Keep observation, sourced fact, interpretation, inference, causal claim and author opinion distinct. A source count is not an argument. A repost is not a purchase; a market-cap snapshot is not invested capital or realized profit; a quote from one participant does not establish the motive of an entire audience. Use the equivalent domain-specific distinction in other subjects.

Do not invent internal motives, missing events, causal relationships, audience consensus or outcomes. Where sources conflict, preserve the discrepancy or use a defensible bounded description rather than manufacturing precision.


## Research and argument construction: select methods by the actual uncertainty

**Discovery versus deep research.** Before committing a thesis, map the question's relevant concepts, adjacent disciplines, known explanations, historic and recent cases, and significant unknowns. Seek the nearest competing published argument rather than treating an unfamiliar idea as original. Select the information gap by reader need, feasibility, evidence and distinctive contribution. After selecting a thesis, decompose its material claims and repeat targeted discovery and deep study wherever a claim depends on a new discipline, premise, case or source. An initial topic search is not completion.

For evidence-dependent articles, keep a compact search-coverage record: question, source classes and time window, search terms including contrary terminology, important inaccessible sources, and whether further work could change a material decision. Inspect original methods, definitions, denominators, comparison groups, results and limitations when those determine the inference. Different accounts of the same underlying report are not independent evidence. Separate fact-checking a statement from deciding what follows from it. Reduce the claim, reject the premise or withhold publication when consequential evidence remains missing. For an experiential essay, prioritize truthful firsthand observations and consent/rights; do not require a literature review merely because the article is long.

**Claim–grounds–warrant stress test.** Use this on material causal, predictive, normative or strategic arguments. Write the precise claim, direct evidence, bridge assumption connecting them, backing for that assumption, strongest rival explanation and scope. Construct a counterexample in which the evidence is true but the conclusion false. If the counterexample survives, the draft must not present the stronger claim as established. In the absence of material inference (e.g. instructions for a known interface), do not force an argument. For evidence-dependent claims, compare the intended reader's prior knowledge and the strongest existing explanation; name the incremental contribution and the conditions under which it disappears.

**Early falsifier.** Before investing in polished prose or expensive assets, test a representative high-risk part: one inference, a case with its alternative explanation, a concrete opening, or an actionable example. Use actual source passages, calculations, experiments or a reader result when available. The sample may fail and return to the question, not simply get a lower score. Do not assert proof of reader demand from a synthetic review. Where an article gives practical advice, inspect prerequisites, a worked example, failure signals, resource burden and alternatives; an explanation-only article need not append a tutorial.

**Professional method references** (adapted as candidates, not evidence of SMS efficacy):
- [Purdue OWL: Toulmin Argument](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/academic_writing/historical_perspectives_on_argumentation/toulmin_argument.html): isolate the warrant; qualifiers and rebuttal as needed, not a universal paragraph format.
- [Cochrane Handbook 6.5, chapter 4](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-04): topic-appropriate databases, eligibility-driven coverage, alternative source types and documentation reduce selection gaps. Adapt the search principle; do not impose a medical systematic-review protocol.
- [Reuters standards](https://reutersagency.com/about/standards-values/): source honesty, attribution, correction and independence. Preserve the distinction between reporting and a signed editorial point of view.
- [UW–Madison reverse outlining](https://writing.wisc.edu/handbook/processandstructure/reverseoutlines/): inspect the actual draft's main points to diagnose order, repetition and missing transitions. Reference was discoverable, but full page fetch failed in the present review.
- [Kamoi et al., TACL 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.78/): distinguish self-generated critique from feedback grounded in external checks; the survey is not evidence that an extra reviewer guarantees better writing.

## Structure follows the material

Compare genuinely different story engines when more than one is plausible: conflict, reversal, anomaly, case, consequence chain, investigation, personal observation or another structure supported by the material. Choose what best serves the reader and argument. Named templates are candidates, not mandatory shells.

For each major section, identify its reader question, new information, evidence or example, consequence and payoff. Avoid sections that merely announce importance, repeat the previous section or narrate the article's outline.

**Paragraph-state test:** after each paragraph or section, state what the reader now knows, feels or can infer that they could not before. If removing it changes nothing important, it may be decorative, repetitive or misplaced.

When the material supports a causal or emotional progression, test the sequence explicitly: facts and context → reversal or contradiction → mechanism → differentiated consequences → human or practical cost → constructive implication or earned payoff. This is a candidate pattern, not a required structure; do not force it onto unrelated material.

## Drafting

- Begin with a concrete fact, scene, case, conflict, observation or contradiction when supported.
- State a real point of view. Research should strengthen the argument, not replace it with a report.
- Explain the positive mechanism or central claim directly before adding defensive qualifications.
- Use examples only when each tests a distinct part of the thesis. Do not turn cases into a catalogue.
- Use counterexamples that genuinely challenge the thesis and force a boundary or refinement; do not add decorative balance.
- Make comparisons explain why one path differs from another when that distinction matters.
- Close the central question with a usable distinction, mental model, implication, test or bounded uncertainty.
- Let the material determine paragraph length and rhythm. Do not impose arbitrary word counts, sentence quotas, slang or punctuation tricks unless the owner requires them.
- Use the strongest available owner-approved voice evidence. Humanizing by replacing phrases is not a writing method.

## Cold-reader review and ablation

Read without defending the author's intent. Mark where the reader loses the question, encounters repeated information, receives no new value, or is promised a payoff that never arrives. Test whether each section changes the reader's understanding.

Remove or rebuild material whose page cost is not justified. Specifically test for repeated disclaimers, defensive asides, rhetorical scaffolding and repeated “not X but Y” constructions that do not advance the argument. Retain qualifications required to prevent factual overreach; do not remove them merely to sound confident.

A curiosity gap is valid only when concrete, relevant and honestly paid off. Clicks alone do not prove satisfaction or completion. A constructive action or experiment can strengthen a critical article when it follows from the argument, but do not bolt on generic advice to every piece.


## Version-bound editorial findings and recursive re-entry

Use the actual manuscript as the review object, not the intended outline. For a material finding, retain the exact draft version, section or upstream decision, observed problem, source or test reference, affected promise, severity, proposed root and what would falsify it. Reopen the earliest contradicted dependency: audience/purpose, novelty or thesis, research or warrant, narrative order, paragraph, style, translation, visual or publishing handoff. Preserve unaffected work. An editor may challenge a reviewer's root diagnosis; resolve a finding only after actual correction and readback, with a reference to the correction. Reviewer opinion and measured effect remain separate.

For durable or risk-heavy articles, the existing article JSON inspector supports an optional `editorial_trace` declaration. Bind it with `article_digest(article)` from `social_media_studio.domain.longform_evidence`, then run `sms-studio inspect-editorial ARTICLE.json --kind article`. The digest covers the JSON contract excluding the trace; it does not identify a separate rendered HTML or Markdown file. Nodes declare ID, purpose, state (OPEN, CANDIDATE, SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED), dependency IDs, evidence references, and relevant declared claim/section IDs. Findings declare ID, target ID, problem, evidence references, severity BLOCKING/ADVISORY, state OPEN/RESOLVED and a resolution reference when resolved. Externally detected source or decision revisions must be passed as changed_node_ids. The inspector checks graph validity, declared coverage, stale article fingerprint and transitive affected nodes. A consistent trace is still REVIEW_PENDING; no authenticity, semantic correctness, independent review, publication or method promotion is asserted. Do not force a trace on an ordinary brief essay or short copy. Direct chat can keep the same material decisions and evidence in a lightweight work packet without claiming a local runtime executed.

Review the full manuscript independently when available, without exposing the author's intended explanation or score. A cold reader should be able to restate the central idea and the actual payoff in their own words. Expert review may be needed for a consequential domain claim; verify the source directly, not the writer's description. A separate model is auxiliary, not automatically independent. Real reader response is evidence of comprehension or adoption under its observed conditions, not proof of factual truth. With no independent reviewer, keep that obligation pending and deliver only an honestly labeled draft.

**Learning closure.** Record scoped failures, the smallest counterexample, actual before/after changes, what worked, what did not, owner corrections and burden/cost as project-local evidence when authorized. Retain domain claims as dated semantic knowledge; retain candidate procedures separately with triggers, inputs, operation, output, falsifier, limits, fallback and replay record. The existing self-learning protocol owns promotion and retirement. A synthetic regression checks mechanical behavior only; two distinct real replays, independent review, counterexample, transfer and rollback evidence are still required. No private author records belong in a public repository.


## Knowledge-to-work feedback (conditional trial, not a gate)

Resolve the current owner goal through the existing entry and private method index; read the actual selected method, not only its title or summary. For a consequential uncertainty, compare the method's scope, source revision, negative case and alternatives against the current task. Apply the useful part while making the decision or draft, not just during a retrospective checklist. Record material observations in the existing project-local work packet or direct-session casebook: exact method reference/revision, current question and applicability, consulted-only / changed decision / retained after a concrete test / rejected, specific prior decision or passage and resulting decision or passage, evidence and outcome/unknown. A method may appropriately confirm a good decision; do not manufacture edits to prove activity. Do not create another ledger, universal stage or record per sentence. Check the actual before/after artifact and original source; assess quality separately from use. If source, constraints or premise changes, reopen dependent observations; do not reuse stale outcomes. Record explicit negative cases and owner burden, and keep the candidate unpromoted until the existing self-learning evidence ladder passes.

For complex work already using the optional `editorial_trace`, `method_observations` can declare id, target_id, method_ref, method_sha256 (SHA-256 of the consulted method bytes), disposition (CONSULTED_ONLY, CHANGE_DECLARED, RETAINED_AFTER_TEST, REJECTED), reason, evidence_refs; changes/retentions also declare before, after and result_ref, and retentions include test_ref. The inspector only validates these declarations and marks affected target observations REOPEN_REQUIRED. It cannot authenticate the source, observe independent review, establish that a method caused an improvement, or prove that a client selected the Skill. Use the existing changed_node_ids for externally detected revisions. In ordinary chat, retain only the material evidence needed for the selected task; no JSON is required.

## X Article package and visual handoff

Treat the X Article and its distribution assets as separate deliverables with separate jobs:

- Article title: a truthful reason to open.
- Article body: the complete argument and reader journey.
- Cover: the acquisition promise and central visual idea.
- Inline images: distinct narrative or explanatory functions tied to exact article anchors.
- Teaser post: a compact reason to enter the Article, not a duplicate opening paragraph.
- Thread, pinned reply and CTA: separate products only when requested and useful.

Do not treat an X Article as a long thread. Evaluate semantic duplication across title, cover, teaser and body, not only exact-string duplication.

Each inline image should have a unique reader function and a precise article anchor. If moving it to another section does not weaken the article, the image may be generic or insufficiently bound to the argument. Visual execution, returned-file inspection, mobile readability and X-native composer preview belong to their own applicable visual and delivery checks; a local HTML preview is not proof that X preserves the same formatting or crop.

A local web preview/copy handoff can be useful for substantial articles when it materially improves reading, title/body separation or transfer into the publishing interface. Keep the editable manuscript and publishing copy as separate artifacts.

## Language and localization

Choose the primary publication language from the current owner goal and platform. When English is the primary publication artifact, develop the argument in English first and localize it into idiomatic Chinese (or another requested language) by preserving the reasoning, tone and factual boundaries rather than mechanically matching sentence structure. Do not impose English-first authoring when the owner requests another primary language.
