# Long-form Editorial

> Status: CANDIDATE_REQUIRES_REVALIDATION. This method adapts reader-value and narrative-architecture mechanisms from sibling systems. It does not import finance-specific evidence rules or fiction-specific story formulas.

## Scope

Long-form articles, essays, X Articles and other substantial editorial pieces. Do not apply this structure to a one-line caption or turn an article into a video-production pipeline.

## Reader contract before prose

Define the target reader and their job; the article promise and central question; the author's actual thesis or point of view; a concrete opening anchor; the information gap that makes the piece worth entering; an early payoff that proves substance; and a later payoff that makes completion worthwhile.

The article should give a real reader a reason to enter, a clear path through the material, repeated information gain and an ending that pays off the promise.

## Structure follows the material

Compare genuinely different story engines when more than one is plausible: conflict, reversal, anomaly, case, consequence chain, investigation, personal observation or another structure supported by the material. Choose what best serves the reader and argument. Named templates are candidates, not mandatory shells.

For each major section, identify its reader question, new information, evidence or example, consequence and payoff. Avoid sections that merely announce importance, repeat the previous section or narrate the article's outline.

## Drafting

- Begin with a concrete fact, scene, case, conflict, observation or contradiction when supported.
- State a real point of view. Research should strengthen the argument, not replace it with a report.
- Use examples and counterexamples to clarify the mechanism and test the thesis.
- Make comparisons explain why one path differs from another when that distinction matters.
- Close the central question with a usable distinction, mental model, implication or bounded uncertainty.
- Let the material determine paragraph length and rhythm. Do not impose arbitrary sentence quotas, slang or punctuation tricks.
- Use the strongest available owner-approved voice evidence. Humanizing by replacing phrases is not a writing method.

## Cold-reader review

Read without defending the author's intent. Mark where the reader loses the question, encounters repeated information, receives no new value, or is promised a payoff that never arrives. Test whether each section changes the reader's understanding. Remove or rebuild material whose page cost is not justified.

A curiosity gap is valid only when concrete, relevant and honestly paid off. Clicks alone do not prove satisfaction or completion.
