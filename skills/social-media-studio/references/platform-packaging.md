# Platform Packaging and Localization

> Status: CANDIDATE_REQUIRES_REVALIDATION. This reference owns platform-field composition and localization, not the underlying creative idea or image-generation executor.

## Scope

Use when the owner requests a platform package, platform-native variants, title, description, tags, cover copy or platform-spec research. Select only the fields requested.

## Give every field a distinct job

- **Title/headline:** earn entry with a truthful promise or point of tension.
- **Description/caption:** add context, voice, interpretation or a useful detail not already performed by the title.
- **Cover copy:** create a fast visual reason to stop or click. It is not a duplicate title.
- **Hashtags:** identify relevant subject/community/discovery context. Do not pad a package to hit an arbitrary count.
- **Call to action:** invite one meaningful next action only when the content and account have earned it. Do not append generic like/share/comment/follow requests by default.

Exact-string duplication can be checked mechanically; semantic duplication requires an editorial read. A structurally valid package is not necessarily compelling.

## Platform-native adaptation

Adapt for the actual surface, format and audience. Do not assume one universal copy length, hook syntax, hashtag count or algorithm rule applies everywhere. Research current official platform documentation when a current character limit, aspect ratio, safe area, feature or publishing rule materially affects the output.

Platform conventions are evidence to consider, not a reason to caricature the platform. Do not claim algorithmic reach or ranking effects without reliable current evidence.

## Localization

When the same content is requested in multiple languages, preserve the underlying subject, creative promise and factual boundary while adapting idiom, sentence rhythm, humor and cultural references. Literal translation is not localization. Do not invent separate channel strategies unless the owner asks.

## Cover relationship

Cover copy and visual concept should reinforce the title without repeating it. The image concept may be an art-directed interpretation rather than a literal frame from the source, but the subject, visual promise and text must remain faithful to the actual content. Visual design and image generation are separate capabilities; a packaging contract does not imply an image has been generated.

## Acceptance

Verify requested fields, language, platform, current specifications where relevant, semantic non-redundancy, truthful promise, tag relevance and CTA necessity. Keep creative quality, platform-spec verification, publication authority and measured performance as separate states.
