# CAOS-governed Video Studio handoff — executable boundary (candidate)

Optional cross-boundary admission contract, not a second CAOS runtime, task ledger, permission grant, or evidence of native ChatGPT Skill selection.

## Scope

Owner-selected CAOS child work creates a Video Studio brief with governance.mode = CAOS_PARENT, governance.parent_system = cognition-agent-os, and a declared allowed_effects array. Direct standalone Video projects omit the governance field. Video owns its business IDs, tasks, artifacts, rights and release authority.

## Admission before claim

The parent recovers goal and corrections, researches primary sources, compares causes and alternatives, challenges the proposed method, checks effects authorization and specifies independent owner-outcome acceptance. It passes a JSON receipt to the child via the admit-parent-handoff CLI. Its schema_version is 1, parent_system is cognition-agent-os, child_system is video-growth-agent, and project_id is exact.

The receipt must bind brief_sha256 (canonical JSON, sorted keys and compact separators, UTF-8 SHA-256), owner_goal_ref, exact brief goal, corrections, sources, competing hypotheses with discriminators, alternatives and selected option, falsifier, rollback, allowed effects within the brief's scope, outcome/evidence conditions, and unknowns.

The native StudioEngine rejects any task claim without current admission for CAOS_PARENT projects. A changed brief digest stales the receipt. Brief revision cannot silently downgrade CAOS_PARENT into standalone. release-check fails on missing/stale handoff. The ledger retains exact accepted receipts; re-admission of identical current receipt is idempotent.

## What remains unproven

The gate checks structural completeness and source binding, not whether the sources, owner reference, decision or permissions are truthful. It does not verify native ChatGPT Skill selection or independently prove meaningful owner outcomes. All of these require independent source/owner readback and native first-mile tests. A child project falsely created as VIDEO_STANDALONE remains an unblocked bypass: this must be addressed at the actual ChatGPT first-mile routing/host gateway, not disguised as solved here.

## Competing routes and falsifier

Prompt-only repetition previously failed to ensure CAOS use. Forcing all Video work onto the CAOS runtime would break independent standalone Video and introduce unrequired dependencies. This candidate binds an expressly governed project to the existing Video executor without duplicating the controller. It is falsified if governed projects can claim without fresh receipts, or if real opt-in tasks routinely start as standalone. Test both positive and negative real ChatGPT entries before readiness. Do not equate static tests or CI with product acceptance.
